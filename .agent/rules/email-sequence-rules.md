# 📧 Email Sequence Design Rules

Guidelines for creating email sequences that nurture relationships, drive action, and achieve conversion goals.

## 1. Core Principles

- **One Email, One Job**: Each email has one primary purpose and one main CTA. Don't try to do everything in a single message.
- **Value Before Ask**: Lead with usefulness, build trust through content, and earn the right to sell.
- **Relevance Over Volume**: Fewer, better emails win. Segment for relevance. Quality > frequency.
- **Clear Path Forward**: Every email moves recipients somewhere useful with obvious next steps.

## 2. Sequence Strategy

### Sequence Length Guidelines

| Sequence Type | Typical Length | Timing Between Emails |
| ------------- | -------------- | --------------------- |
| Welcome       | 3-7 emails     | 1-2 days              |
| Lead Nurture  | 5-10 emails    | 2-4 days              |
| Onboarding    | 5-10 emails    | 1-3 days              |
| Re-engagement | 3-5 emails     | 2-3 days              |
| Post-purchase | 3-5 emails     | 2-7 days              |

### Subject Line Strategy

- Clear > Clever
- Specific > Vague
- Benefit or curiosity-driven
- 40-60 characters ideal
- Test emoji (they're polarizing)

**Patterns that work:**

- Question: "Still struggling with X?"
- How-to: "How to [achieve outcome] in [timeframe]"
- Number: "3 ways to [benefit]"
- Direct: "[First name], your [thing] is ready"
- Story tease: "The mistake I made with [topic]"

### Preview Text

- Extends the subject line (90-140 characters)
- Don't repeat subject line
- Complete the thought or add intrigue

## 3. Email Types Reference

### Onboarding Emails

**New Users Series**

- Trigger: User signs up (free or trial)
- Goal: Activate user, drive to aha moment
- Typical sequence: 5-7 emails over 14 days

**New Customers Series**

- Trigger: User converts to paid
- Goal: Reinforce purchase decision, drive adoption, reduce early churn
- Typical sequence: 3-5 emails over 14 days

**Key Onboarding Step Reminder**

- Trigger: User hasn't completed critical setup step after X time
- Goal: Nudge completion of high-value action
- Format: Single email or 2-3 email mini-sequence

### Retention Emails

**Upgrade to Paid**

- Trigger: Free user shows engagement, or trial ending
- Goal: Convert free to paid
- Typical sequence: 3-5 emails

**Upgrade to Higher Plan**

- Trigger: User approaching plan limits or using features available on higher tier
- Goal: Upsell to next tier
- Format: Single email or 2-3 email sequence

**Ask for Review**

- Trigger: Customer milestone (30/60/90 days, key achievement)
- Goal: Generate social proof
- Format: Single email

**Proactive Support**

- Trigger: Signs of struggle (drop in usage, failed actions)
- Goal: Save at-risk user, improve experience
- Format: Single email

### Billing Emails

**Failed Payment Recovery**

- Trigger: Payment fails
- Goal: Recover revenue, retain customer
- Typical sequence: 3-4 emails over 7-14 days

**Switch to Annual**

- Trigger: Monthly subscriber at renewal time
- Goal: Convert monthly to annual (improve LTV, reduce churn)
- Format: Single email or 2-email sequence

### Win-Back Emails

**Expired Trials**

- Trigger: Trial ended without conversion
- Goal: Convert or re-engage
- Typical sequence: 3-4 emails over 30 days

**Cancelled Customers**

- Trigger: Time after cancellation (30, 60, 90 days)
- Goal: Win back churned customers
- Typical sequence: 2-3 emails spread over 90 days

## 4. Email Copy Guidelines

### Structure

1. **Hook**: First line grabs attention
2. **Context**: Why this matters to them
3. **Value**: The useful content
4. **CTA**: What to do next
5. **Sign-off**: Human, warm close

### Formatting

- Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences)
- White space between sections
- Bullet points for scannability
- Bold for emphasis (sparingly)
- Mobile-first (most read on phone)

### Tone

- Conversational, not formal
- First-person (I/we) and second-person (you)
- Active voice
- Match your brand but lean friendly
- Read it out loud—does it sound human?

### Length

- Shorter is usually better
- 50-125 words for transactional
- 150-300 words for educational
- 300-500 words for story-driven
- If it's long, it better be good

## 5. Personalization & Segmentation

### Merge Fields

- First name (fallback to "there" or "friend")
- Company name (B2B)
- Relevant data (usage, plan, etc.)

### Dynamic Content

- Based on segment
- Based on behavior
- Based on stage

### Segmentation Strategies

**By Behavior:**

- Openers vs. non-openers
- Clickers vs. non-clickers
- Active vs. inactive

**By Stage:**

- Trial vs. paid
- New vs. long-term
- Engaged vs. at-risk

**By Profile:**

- Industry/role (B2B)
- Use case / goal
- Company size

## 6. Testing & Optimization

### What to Test

- Subject lines (highest impact)
- Send times
- Email length
- CTA placement and copy
- Personalization level
- Sequence timing

### Metrics to Track

- Open rate (benchmark: 20-40%)
- Click rate (benchmark: 2-5%)
- Unsubscribe rate (keep under 0.5%)
- Conversion rate (specific to sequence goal)
- Revenue per email (if applicable)
